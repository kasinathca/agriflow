# Reproducible pipeline

```text
official source or export
  -> retained raw response/file + SHA-256 provenance
  -> schema normalization and stable market identity
  -> deterministic duplicate handling and market-day aggregation
  -> coverage and source-authority audit
  -> reviewed coordinates and gridded weather, where available
  -> readiness gates
  -> checksum-protected empirical freeze
  -> lead-lag, influence, integration, geography, weather and crunch analyses
  -> dashboard and review evidence
```

Missing mandi reports are not converted to zero, and price-network inference does not
forward-fill across reporting gaps. Price leadership means statistical temporal precedence,
not causal or commercial control.

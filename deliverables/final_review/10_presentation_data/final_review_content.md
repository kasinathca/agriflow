# Final-review presentation content status

This is a factual structure for a 10–15 slide review. Current results use REAL CEDA data but
remain exploratory because statewide coverage, coordinates, weather and a final freeze are incomplete.

1. **Title** — AgriFlow: price transmission and market integration across Indian mandis.
2. **Problem statement** — fragmented reporting and uneven spatial/temporal market integration.
3. **Objectives** — audit coverage; infer lead-lag associations; compare integration,
   geography, weather context and arrival-price crunches.
4. **Proposed solution** — provenance-gated ingestion, reproducible analytics and a local Dash
   demonstrator.
5. **Dataset and preparation** — 8,499 raw rows, 7,794 market-days, 25 markets, four
   Maharashtra districts, 82.10% arrival coverage, and explicit unknown arrival units.
6. **Methodology** — daily log returns, overlap/effect thresholds, FDR, source-oriented
   influence, integration, QAP distance diagnostic and declustered weather event study.
7. **Analytical implementation** — emphasize association and temporal precedence, not causality.
8. **Results and evaluation** — current exploratory tables contain six significant lead-lag
   edges, eight influence rows, 42 integration pairs and 34 crunch candidates; do not present
   these as causal or statewide findings before a passing final freeze.
9. **Application** — verified Overview, choropleth, influence/ripple, weather/crunch,
   geography, quality, research-status and source views.
10. **Challenges and solutions** — reporting gaps, GeoJSON names, identity, arrivals units,
    provenance and credentialed access.
11. **Conclusion and future scope** — only after empirical readiness passes.
12. **References** — OGD/AGMARKNET, CEDA, NASA POWER, geoBoundaries/ADM1, IMD and declared
    software dependencies.
